# October web review pool

Collected 112 publicly accessible articles into private storage on 4 October
2026. The 180 excerpts contain 31,497 words. No model predictions or labels were
used in URL selection, collection, overlap screening or split allocation. This
is additional review material, not a scored test set or ready-to-use training data.

`expand_web_sources.py` selected previously unused article-shaped URLs from the
saved 30 September public index snapshots. It excluded the earlier 90 selected
URLs, then used a fixed hash ordering with at most 40 URLs per publisher. The
frozen public source catalog is `web-sources-20261004.json`. Newly collected does
not imply newly published. All raw HTML, extracted text and document split
membership stay outside Git under the private DMZ data directory.

The existing collector enforced robots rules, request delays, response bounds,
redirect restrictions and local provenance hashes. All 112 pages were fetched
and extracted; three were excluded as collection duplicates. Extraction has not
received full human verification. No text was sent to an inference provider.

## Review allocation

Overlap screening includes 33 prior text files, including the unchanged
88-document blind packet. The new pool excludes 56 documents with any normalized
exact 12-gram overlap against that inventory. This conservative rule also removes
documents with shared boilerplate; it does not establish semantic independence.

The remaining documents are grouped by transitive 20% 12-gram containment before
deterministic group assignment. At that threshold all 53 retained documents are
separate groups. Hash buckets allocate nominally 60%/20%/20% to review roles;
these are not labels, and there is no guarantee of balanced strata in small pools.

| Review role | Documents | Excerpts | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Training review | 34 | 58 | 10,478 |
| Development review | 9 | 16 | 3,030 |
| Reserved review | 10 | 18 | 3,301 |

Only two retained excerpts are financial, both in training review; there is no
new legal or mixed-chat coverage. General and public-administration prose dominate.
Additional sources are needed before defining balanced primary evaluation strata.
Do not silently reassign reserved documents after inspecting model predictions.

All 92 retained excerpts have null labels and remain unreviewed. Their split
names end in `-review` so the training loader cannot treat them as `train` rows.
The [aggregate manifest](../results/quality-99-v1/web-review-splits.json) records
source, excerpt and membership hashes. Individual membership and source text are
private. Source-use review remains pending before redistribution.

The earlier 142-excerpt blind packet remains separate and unchanged, with its
original SHA-256 `18e32974981aed65237247926fca5a3a9a7ef3b06287ac1e8cf03e7f1d6919b6`.
Neither pool can support an independent accuracy claim until extraction review,
complete annotations, second review and adjudication are finished.

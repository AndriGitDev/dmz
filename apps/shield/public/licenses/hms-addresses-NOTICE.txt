DMZ Icelandic address vocabulary

The trained Shield alpha bundles a derived street/address-form vocabulary from
HMS Stadfangaskra (Iceland's address register). It contains address forms rather
than household or subscriber records. DMZ transforms approved nominative/dative
forms into a local detection lookup; source address records are not included as
a separate dataset.

Source: HMS, Stadfangaskra
https://hms.is/gogn-og-maelabord/grunngogntilnidurhals/stadfangaskra
https://hmsstgsftpprodweu001.blob.core.windows.net/fasteignaskra/Stadfangaskra.csv

Source and derived vocabulary hashes/counts are recorded in detector-data.json.
This attribution does not establish detection accuracy or imply HMS endorsement.

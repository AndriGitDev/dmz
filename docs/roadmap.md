# dmz roadmap

1. Prepare the extension and website source under GPL-3.0-only, including contribution guidance, security reporting, third-party notices and a publication review.
2. Make basic builds reproducible and clearly distinguish their coverage from trained builds. Review external asset licensing and publish verified source/package identity and checksums for releases.
3. Improve installation, accessibility, local masking, restoration and understandable failure warnings with fabricated-data testing.
4. Obtain independent detector annotations and validation. Existing development measurements and regression fixtures do not establish field quality.
5. Maintain code review, synthetic bug triage, translations and concise release notes without collecting prompts or adding trackers.

No general-release date or guaranteed support level is promised. Preserve recall per entity type on the synthetic regression benchmark; research targets remain unproven. See the [quality contract](detector-quality-contract.md) and [publication checklist](open-source-readiness.md).

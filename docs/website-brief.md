Icelandic is default with an English mirror, canonical URL and hreflang links for each page. Use `/vidbot/` and `/en/extension/` for installation, `/samfelag/` and `/en/community/` for participation, plus trust, reviewed benchmark, about, privacy and license information. Redirect obsolete addresses to relevant current content.

State that host-page scripts can see original drafts before masking, failures warn and continue sending, attachments pass unchanged and misses/context can identify people. Values and mappings used by dmz stay on device. No anonymity, perfect detection or legal-compliance promise.

Keep public detector content to reviewed summary measurements and limitations. Do not copy raw corpora, private research or unlicensed assets into website source or downloads. Preserve self-hosted fonts, accessibility and the no-tracker baseline. Use existing Kastro contact routes and private andri@kastro.is security reports; collect no real prompts.

GPL licensing allows source review and reuse subject to its terms. It does not prevent malicious copies. Explain official source/release identity without claiming signatures or independent audits that do not exist.

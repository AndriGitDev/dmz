## Primary journey

Understand free DMZ -> inspect availability and installation -> read limits -> participate voluntarily. The owner authorized a trained alpha ZIP under ADR 0006. The primary action is to download the DMZ Chromium extension from its verified artifact path, with size, version, checksum and explicit alpha status. Existing testers can follow instructions for the package supplied by the maintainer. No fake download button, waiting-list prerequisite or commercial intake.

Icelandic is default, with an English mirror for every public route. Preserve canonical and hreflang links, keyboard access, mobile layout and synthetic-only demonstrations. Use existing visual identity, no trackers, third-party fonts, cookies, embeds or external form tools.

## Claims and contact

State DMZ's on-device masking/mapping scope precisely. The original draft is entered into the AI site's composer, whose scripts can see it before masking. Submitted text can contain misses, failure-path originals and unchanged attachments. Warned best-effort continuation is current policy. No anonymity, 100% detection, encryption of visible markers, compliance, certification or provider-ZDR promise.

Free means no DMZ subscription, seat minimum, premium protection tier or mandatory account. The AI service's own account/costs remain separate. Kastro help is optional and quoted separately. Free does not mean open source; code, research and model-disclosure restrictions remain in force.

Use Kastro's established localized contact page and private `andri@kastro.is` security route. Do not advertise unverified `pilot@dmz.is`, `hello@dmz.is` or `security@dmz.is` mailboxes. Do not transmit prompts or real examples as part of feedback. Public feedback template is bilingual and collects only voluntary fabricated-data reports.

Final operator, hosting-log/retention, contact-retention and distribution disclosures still require verification before general release. Cite actual evidence for public measured claims, retaining independent-validation limitations.

Old `/skjoldur/` and `/en/shield/` addresses redirect to the renamed extension guide. Serve the large test ZIP and checksum through the persistent `/downloads/` volume; keep binary/model assets out of Git and never replace the trained alpha with a basic package silently.

# Financial inputs and arithmetic check

Status — 27 October 2026: historical commercial baseline. The [free community concept](community-project.md) supersedes commercial priorities, pricing and paid-pilot/revenue gates. Retained for provenance; not the active delivery plan or evidence of current availability.

The 27 September 2026 plan refers to an embedded quarterly model that was not included in the supplied text. This document checks the figures derivable from the stated prices and year-end customer counts, and marks those that require the missing model.

## Recurring account assumptions

| Segment | Formula | MRR/account |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Small firm | 30 × €6 Shield | €180 |
| Regulated mid-size | 100 × €6 Shield + 20 × €18 Portal | €960 |
| Vendor | 15 × €25 Gateway + 15 × €6 Shield | €465 |

The regulated example assumes the 20 Portal seats are **additional billable seats** on top of the 100 Shield seats. Because the Portal plan says it “includes Shield,” confirm whether the example really represents 120 users or double-counts 20 users. At 80 Shield + 20 Portal, MRR would be €840, materially lowering the base case.

## Figures requiring the missing quarterly model

The stated annual revenue (€73k, €236k, €449k), costs (€192k, €353k, €449k), Q3 2029 first profitable quarter, €261k Q2 2029 peak cash need and €602k end-2029 downside loss cannot be independently reconstructed from year-end customer counts alone. They depend on quarterly acquisition timing, onboarding fees, model usage, payment timing and possibly churn. Obtain the source spreadsheet and record every formula before investor or grant use.

The downside halves the base-case end customer count to 49 by 2029 while holding costs constant; this does not by itself establish a €602k cumulative loss without the quarter-by-quarter schedule.

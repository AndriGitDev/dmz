# ADR 0005: Free community project, Shield first

Status: accepted owner direction · 27 October 2026

## Decision

DMZ becomes a free community project like Dmark and Kastro Lookup. Shield is the initial product. No DMZ subscriptions, premium security tier, seat minimum, paid-pilot programme or mandatory account is part of this scope. Optional Kastro implementation/training work is separate and never gates useful local functionality.

Portal/Gateway are deferred without a delivery commitment. Commercial validation, revenue milestones and certification expansion targets in the September plan are historical. ADR 0004 remains the technical constraint if hosted work is explicitly revived; it is not an active build instruction.

## Consequences

Use the [community concept](../community-project.md), [roadmap](../roadmap.md) and [release checklist](../launch-checklist.md). Align both website languages, old public URLs, extension identity, distribution guidance and Kastro references. Do not change detector policy, weights or measured results merely to change positioning.

Free access does not authorize public source/model publication or dataset redistribution. The product and research remain private pending explicit licensing decisions. Maintain reviewed summary-only detector disclosure. Existing quality and regression gates remain applicable, and independent validation remains incomplete.

Shield continues its 9 October best-effort policy, including warned failure continuation and unchanged attachments. The original draft is visible to the host site's composer before masking; do not claim pre-submit isolation or a complete enforcement boundary.
